Who We Are

A Legacy of
Creative Excellence

Sneha Enterprises is a full-service, 360-degree advertising firm founded in 2001 in Mumbai. Over 25 remarkable years, we have evolved from a traditional mainline agency into a powerhouse of integrated marketing — blending the best of classic advertising with cutting-edge digital solutions.

We are not restricted to any particular vertical. From real estate giants and FMCG leaders to tech startups and luxury brands, our portfolio spans every industry. We believe every brand has a unique story — and we are here to tell it in the most impactful way possible.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2001
The Beginning
Sneha Enterprises was founded in Borivali, Mumbai, with a vision to redefine 360-degree advertising through creativity and a client-first approach.
2007
Expansion into Print & Outdoor
Established partnerships with major print publications and outdoor media houses, becoming a go-to agency for billboard and transit advertising across Mumbai.
2014
Digital Transformation
Launched our dedicated digital marketing division, embracing social media, performance marketing, and modern digital branding.
2020
100+ Clients Milestone
Crossed the landmark of serving 100+ satisfied clients, including leading brands like Sony, Tanishq, Big Bazaar, Croma, and Tata Sky.
2026
25 Years of Excellence
Celebrating 25 years of accelerating brands with impactful creativity — now powered by AI-driven insights and next-generation marketing tools.
